Color rules

Do — use pink only for actions and selected states; keep promo/decorative bands navy→blue; put a 1px white border on any primary button that sits over a gradient; use solid indigo/navy cards on white pages.
Don’t — reuse the CTA gradient as a background; use pink for large surfaces; make text translucent white on light gradients (add a black/30 scrim or darken the surface until white text hits 4.5:1).

Accessibility floor

Contrast

  • White text must reach 4.5 : 1 on its background (WCAG AA).
  • Light or gradient backgrounds get a black/30 scrim (promo bands, rating and amenities cards).
  • The mobile confirmation receipt tints itself from the photo, then darkens until white text passes 4.5 : 1.

Controls & semantics

  • Real buttons, labels and inputs; semantic footer / nav / lists.
  • Tap targets ≥ 43 px tall on mobile primary actions.
  • Language toggle is a labeled button pair; choice persists.
  • Respect reduced-motion for any animated band.

Mobile rules

Flow & footer

  • Room type, date, time, payment and new card all return to Checkout through URL params.
  • Screen = min-h-[100dvh] block, compact footer below it (no columns, small logo).

Rows & heights

  • Checkout: guest 60 · room 50 · check-in/out 125 · price 109 · payment 44 · promo 44.
  • Room-type rows 37; selected = pink-highlight 0.5px border + 40% fill.
  • Primary buttons: 43 (checkout, save card) · 48 pill (pickers) · 50 (payment).

Special cases

  • VISA badge stays Visa navy #1A1F71 — a card-network mark, not brand color (intentional).
  • Confirmation receipt tints from its photo; text must still hit 4.5 : 1.
  • Menu drawer uses the translucent pink→indigo menu gradient and holds the EN/ES row.

Mobile confirmation & new card

Brand, language & rules

The decisions that keep the system consistent as it grows.

Logo & favicon

White logo on indigo, navy or photo scrim

Standard logo on white / light

“P” mark — favicon 16/32/48 + touch icon

  • Header logo heights: 35 (map), 40 (Navbar), 48 (Home hero).
  • Never recolor the wordmark; keep clear space ≥ logo height / 2.

Language

Default & toggle

  • English by default for everyone; no location-based detection.
  • EN/ES toggle in every desktop header, Confirmation Navbar and the mobile menu (“🌍 Language”).
  • Choice persists (localStorage key popnest_language).

Content

  • ~180 strings × 2 languages, one flat dictionary; workspace data stores keys, not text.
  • Icons look up by stable id, never by translated label.

Layout for Spanish

  • Expect ~25% longer strings — allow wrap; buttons size to content or fill available width.
  • Test every screen in both languages before shipping.

Golden rules

1 · One primary action per screen — the CTA gradient, nowhere else.
2 · Roles, not preferences — pink = action/selection; indigo/navy = surface; white = text.
3 · Separate decoration from action — promo/decorative bands never share a hue with the CTA.
4 · Contrast is non-negotiable — 4.5 : 1 for white text, fixed with scrims, not with opacity.
5 · Tokens once — colors live in the theme, gradients in one file; no hex in components.
6 · Explain exceptions — Visa navy, square reservation fields and lavender tag are deliberate; document any new one.
